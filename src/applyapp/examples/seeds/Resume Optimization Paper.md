# Resume optimization paper (example)

Craft notes shipped with ApplyApp so a new install has an ATS seed. Replace this with your own rules. The filename must contain "optimization paper" or "resume optimization".

- Use the headings SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, and EDUCATION.
- Lead each bullet with an action and a result that appears in the accomplishments file.
- Do not add a job title under the contact line.
- Keep the resume to two pages.
